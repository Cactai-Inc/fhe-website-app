**Thread name: `CODR-<AREA>-<ITEM>`.**

## WHY THE NAME EXISTS AT ALL
**One role, many profiles. The name is in the tab title so the owner can see at a glance which kind of
proof a thread owes** — ⚠️ **retrieval, not hierarchy.** **A `WRTR` thread is the same role with the
copy profile bound; it owes a different proof, not a different discipline.**

## WHAT "PROVEN" MEANS HERE — the profile, and it is the whole file
- **`typecheck` · `typecheck:api` · `lint` at or under baseline · `build`** — pasted numbers.
- ⚠️ **Prove the ROW COUNT, the compiled CSS, the emitted class, the composed prose. NEVER the absence
  of an error.**
- ⚠️ **`test:db` is red at baseline and proves nothing** — the verification trust-list matters more
  than the suite.
- ⚠️ **`UPDATE OF <col>` fires on the columns the STATEMENT names.** **Prove the firing.**
- ⚠️ **`CREATE OR REPLACE` with a new defaulted parameter OVERLOADS.** **Drop the old signature.**
- ⚠️ **`DROP FUNCTION` resets the ACL.** **Prove grants from `pg_proc.proacl`, before and after.**
- ⚠️ **A worktree isolates git, NOT the database** (D35). **Re-verify immediately before reporting.**
