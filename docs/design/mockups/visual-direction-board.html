FHE App · Visual direction board · pick one, I build everything in it

Three ways this app can look — same components, three worlds

Each direction renders the identical hero, decision card, and KPI panel so the comparison is honest. The marketing site keeps its heritage brand either way — this chooses the product's own language. Shape variety, gradients, and light are structural in all three; none of them is a wall of green rounded rectangles.

Direction A

First Light

★ My recommendation

Dawn over the arena. Near-white canvas, deep pine kept only as the action color (re-tuned brighter, always as a gradient, never a flat slab), and the signature is atmospheric light: a dawn gradient — soft gold into rose into sky — that appears as glow behind heroes, gradient borders on what's live, and the progress rings. Brass replaces gold as a hairline accent. Serif (Fraunces) reserved for numerals and the greeting. Heritage survives as a thread; light does the sophistication.

Direction B

Tack Room

The horse world's own materials: saddle leather, oxblood, brass, porcelain. Warm without being parchment — espresso ink and deep leather gradients give it weight, stitched hairlines and warm shadow give it craft. The one direction where dark surfaces appear, and only in heroes, lit by a cognac glow instead of sitting flat. Richest, most unmistakably equestrian; the risk is warmth reading heavy if over-applied, so it lives on discipline.

Direction C

Show Season

Competition-day crispness: pure white, graphite ink, and one vivid signature — the ribbon gradient, cobalt into violet, the colors of championship ribbons — carried as an edge stripe, live-state text, and data fills. All-Inter, tightest tracking, circles for people, bars for progress. The most contemporary-SaaS of the three and the furthest from the heritage brand; nothing about it is drab, and nothing about it is a barn either.
