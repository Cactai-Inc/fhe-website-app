Downscaled offline by repeated halving rather than one big resize — that averages every source pixel into the result instead of skipping some, which is exactly how fine grain turns to shimmer. The band keeps the centre 45% of the hide's height: with cover on a wide header you only ever see a horizontal slice through the middle (~33% of it on a phone, ~10% on desktop), so nothing visible is lost and the left/right tanning you liked is horizontal — untouched.
